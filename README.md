# 🛒 CESIM Retail Simulation – Team Orange

### Strategic Performance Analysis of Retail Operations, Pricing & Profitability

This project presents a strategic analysis of **Team Orange's performance in the CESIM Retail Simulation** across six decision-making rounds.

The simulation involved making interconnected retail-management decisions related to **pricing, promotions, advertising, labour management, inventory, category management, and profitability** under changing market conditions.

The project demonstrates how a structured, data-driven strategy can improve retail performance by aligning operational decisions with customer demand, market conditions, and profitability objectives.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The CESIM Retail Simulation provides a dynamic retail environment where strategic decisions directly influence sales, costs, profitability, and competitive performance.

Team Orange's objective was to:

- Maximize profitability
- Improve operational efficiency
- Respond to changing market conditions
- Optimize pricing and promotions
- Manage labour costs
- Improve product-category performance
- Balance demand generation with profitability
- Use market outlooks to support decision-making

The analysis evaluates how the team's strategy evolved across six simulation rounds.

## 🧠 Strategic Framework

The project focused on five major decision areas:

### 1. Pricing Strategy

Pricing decisions were adjusted according to market conditions and customer demand.

- Early rounds focused on reducing prices to stimulate demand
- Later rounds introduced selective price increases
- Pricing decisions considered demand sensitivity and margin improvement

### 2. Demand Generation

Promotional and advertising decisions were used to improve customer traffic and sales.

- Targeted discounts
- Advertising investment
- External promotions
- In-store promotions

### 3. Labour Optimization

Labour decisions focused on controlling costs while maintaining appropriate staffing levels.

- Reduced temporary labour hours
- Improved workforce productivity
- Adjusted staffing according to demand conditions

### 4. Category Management

## 🔄 Six-Round Strategy Evolution

The team's strategy evolved from an initially reactive approach toward a more structured and analytical strategy.

### Round 1–2: Initial Stage

The team faced:

- Declining demand
- High labour costs
- Low profitability
- Limited strategic coordination

The initial results highlighted the need for stronger alignment between pricing, demand generation, and operational efficiency.

### Round 3: Correction Phase

Key actions included:

- Reducing labour costs
- Increasing advertising
- Introducing targeted discounts

**Outcome:** Profitability stabilized and the decline in sales slowed.

### Round 4: Recovery Phase

The team responded to increasing customer traffic by:

- Focusing on high-demand categories
- Improving the store experience
- Adjusting operational decisions

**Outcome:** Sales and profitability began to recover.

### Round 5: Growth Phase

The team capitalized on stronger market demand by:

- Increasing sales volume
- Focusing on food service and beverages
- Maintaining control over operational costs

**Outcome:** Sales increased to approximately **€570,000** with approximately **€80,000** in net income.

### Round 6: Optimization Phase

During peak demand conditions, the team focused on profitability optimization through:

- Selective price increases
- Improved advertising allocation
- Labour optimization
- Greater focus on profitable categories

**Outcome:** Approximately **€600,000 in sales** and **€92,000 in profit**, representing the strongest financial performance in the six-round analysis.

## 📈 Key Performance Drivers

The analysis identified four major drivers of improved performance:

### Labour Cost Reduction
Reducing unnecessary temporary labour improved cost efficiency and profitability.

### Pricing Adjustments
Pricing decisions were gradually shifted from demand stimulation toward margin optimization as market conditions improved.

### Advertising
Advertising helped increase customer inflow, although the analysis also identified inefficiencies in early-round spending.

### Category Focus
Greater emphasis on beverages, food service, and general merchandise improved the overall revenue and profitability mix.

---

## 📊 Category Performance

The final-stage analysis identified different strategic roles for the major categories:

| Category | Performance Insight |
|---|---|
| Beverage | High-volume demand driver |
| Food Service | High-margin profit driver |
| General Merchandise | Strong contribution to profitability |
| Grocery | High volume but comparatively lower margin |

This demonstrated the importance of managing product categories based not only on sales volume but also on their contribution to profitability.

---

## 🔍 Key Challenges Identified

The simulation highlighted several challenges:

- Lack of clear strategic direction during the initial rounds
- Inefficient advertising allocation
- Excessive dependence on discounts
- High labour costs
- Underutilization of high-margin categories
- Difficulty predicting demand fluctuations
- Lack of coordination between pricing, advertising, staffing, and inventory decisions

These challenges reinforced the importance of integrated and data-driven retail decision-making.

---

## 💡 Key Learnings

The simulation provided several important strategic insights:

1. **Sales growth alone does not guarantee profitability.**
2. **Pricing decisions must reflect demand conditions and customer sensitivity.**
3. **Advertising should be evaluated based on its contribution to sales and profitability.**
4. **Labour should be aligned with expected customer demand.**
5. **High-margin categories can significantly improve overall profitability.**
6. **Market outlooks can support proactive rather than reactive decision-making.**
7. **Pricing, inventory, staffing, and promotions should be managed as an integrated system.**
8. **Continuous KPI monitoring is essential for identifying performance gaps.**

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

Based on the six-round analysis, the project recommends:

### 1. Strengthen High-Margin Category Focus
Continue prioritizing food service, beverages, and general merchandise while carefully managing lower-margin categories.

### 2. Optimize Pricing Using Demand Elasticity
Use customer sensitivity and market conditions to determine when to stimulate demand through pricing and when to increase margins.

### 3. Improve Advertising ROI
Allocate advertising budgets toward high-demand categories and monitor the resulting sales and profitability impact.

### 4. Maintain Flexible Labour Management
Align staffing levels with customer demand to control labour costs without compromising service quality.

### 5. Use Market Outlooks Proactively
Anticipate demand and cost changes and adjust pricing, inventory, and promotional strategies accordingly.

### 6. Improve Inventory Management
Maintain appropriate inventory levels based on demand forecasts to reduce stockouts, overstocking, and holding costs.

### 7. Integrate Strategic Decisions
Pricing, promotions, staffing, inventory, and category decisions should be coordinated rather than managed independently.

### 8. Continuously Monitor KPIs
Track:

- Sales growth
- Profit
- Profit margin
- Labour efficiency
- Category contribution
- Advertising effectiveness
